Heritage & Atelier

Two Traditions. One Seamless Wardrobe.

SATINOSTITCH was founded on a singular conviction: the modern connoisseur should never have to choose between the poetic grandeur of Indian artisanal weaves and the sharp sartorial refinement of modern European tailoring.

From master Banarasi silk weavers in Varanasi meticulously inserting antique gold zari into 16-kali lehengas, to master patternmakers tailoring fluid liquid satin and sand-washed linen blazers, our atelier bridges two worlds with uncompromising reverence for craft.
