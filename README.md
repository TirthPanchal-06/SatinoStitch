# SATINOSTITCH – Premium Fashion E-Commerce Platform

SATINOSTITCH is a premium fashion e-commerce platform designed to bring traditional Indian ethnic wear and contemporary fashion together through an elegant and seamless online shopping experience.

The platform provides customers with a refined shopping interface to explore fashion collections, discover products, manage wishlists and shopping bags, and place orders. It also includes a dedicated administrative dashboard to manage products, inventory, orders, customers and business activities.

## Key Features

### Customer Website
- Premium and responsive fashion storefront.
- Elegant product listings and collections.
- Individual product detail pages.
- Product image galleries with multiple viewing angles.
- Product colour and size selection.
- Shopping bag and wishlist management.
- Product search and navigation.
- Customer account management.
- Order management.
- Related products and recommendations.

### Admin Dashboard
- Dedicated and secured administrative interface.
- Product and category management.
- Product image and gallery management.
- Inventory and stock monitoring.
- Order management.
- Customer management.
- Sales overview and analytics.
- Business activity monitoring.
- Product performance insights.

## Design Philosophy

SATINOSTITCH follows a Royal Heritage visual identity, combining traditional fashion aesthetics with modern e-commerce design.

- Deep Burgundy / Wine
- Champagne Gold
- Elegant Ivory
- Premium typography
- Clean layouts and refined interactions

## Project Architecture

The platform follows a frontend-backend-database architecture.

- Frontend: Customer-facing shopping website and administrative interface.
- Backend: REST APIs and application business logic.
- Database: PostgreSQL for structured product, customer, order and business data.

## Project Objective

The primary objective of SATINOSTITCH is to develop a professional fashion retail platform that combines premium visual presentation, convenient shopping functionality and centralized business management.

The project is designed with scalability and future enhancements in mind, allowing additional e-commerce capabilities to be introduced as the business grows.
